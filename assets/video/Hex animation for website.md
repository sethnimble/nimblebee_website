**Objective**
I want to create a short animation for the background of the hero section of my website.  the animation will be looped, so it is important that the starting and closing frames are identical. The animation is designed to represent the purpose of my company, which is to bring order to chaos in a business.

**Hexagon motif**
the idea is to have a large number of plastic hexagonal tiles fall onto a dark tabletop in a product shoot setup. the hexes should be made of a matte plastic material. there should be around 40 in total, varying slightly in size.

**Colours**
25% of the hexagons should be #10B981
25% should be #FF595E
the colours of the remaining hexagons should be a random mix of #FAF8F6, #F59E0B, #06B6D4, #E11D74 & #EA580C
the colours should behave naturally as if realistically lit in the product shoot

**Style**
this should be high res, realistic looking physical objects in a real video shoot

**composition**
- the video is designed to act as the background for a deskop hero section and span the full width of the page
- the background will span the entire desktop frame
- on desktop, the hero has text and CTA on the left side of the page. the falling hexagons can appear anywhere in the right 75% of the width of the video. 
- but when the hexagon converges, it should be on the right of the frame (within the right-side half of the frame)

**The animation**
- the starting frame should be just the background without any hex shapes visible. 
- then the hex shapes fall naturally with a weight of gravity into the scene. 
- they all land and come to rest lying flat on the surface. 
- they melt together into pool of liquid plastic
- this forms into a single large hexagonal tile
- the tile rises and floats in the scene, vertically orientated
- this large hexagonal tile should be oriented with vertices top and bottom and straight edges to the left and right
- the colour of this single hexagon is #FF595E matte plastic
- this pulses and shakes slightly before bursting into hundreds of small bright sparks that glitter and fade to leave just the background of the scene. 
- the final frame should be just the background without any hex shapes visible.
