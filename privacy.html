Privacy

This page covers one thing: the NimbleBee website at nimblebee.co.za. We keep what we learn about visitors to what is essential, which for now is nothing.

What this site does

  • It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
  • It runs no analytics and no tracking. We have not decided whether to add analytics. If that changes, this page changes with it.
  • Its fonts are hosted on our own server, so your browser makes no requests to Google or any other font service.
  • It has no contact form, no sign-in and no accounts.

Hosting

The site is served by Vercel. Like any web host, it sees your IP address and the pages you request while it delivers them, and keeps ordinary server logs. We do not use that information ourselves.

If you contact us

The email and WhatsApp buttons are plain links. Nothing is sent from this site. If you choose to write to us, your email provider or WhatsApp handles the message under their own policies. We receive what you send, including your name or number, and we use it to reply to you.

Who is responsible

StudioOne Creative Solutions PTY LTD, trading as NimbleBee.

Your rights and questions

You can ask us what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email seth@nimblebee.co.za. In South Africa, complaints can also go to the Information Regulator.
