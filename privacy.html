Privacy - NimbleWall

NimbleWall is a personal desktop wallpaper I built for my own use. It shows my tasks, the weather and my calendar. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else signs in to it.

What it accesses

With my permission, it reads event details (titles, dates and times) from my own Google Calendar accounts, read-only. It cannot create, change or delete anything.

What happens to that data

It is fetched when the wallpaper is refreshed, shown on the wallpaper, and not stored afterwards. It is not shared with anyone, sold, or used for advertising or anything else.

Google's rules

Use of information from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
